About the Firm

Rooted in Viramgam,
Serving Across India

M A Meman & Co. is a Chartered Accountancy firm registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), providing comprehensive services in GST, Income tax, accounting & book keeping, statutory & tax audits, corporate compliance, and financial advisory.

Operating from Viramgam, the firm serves businesses across manufacturing, trading, services, and the MSME sector, with a focus on accurate compliance, timely filings, and substantive advisory grounded in current law and regulatory developments.

What We Do

Areas of Practice

Our work spans statutory obligations, advisory engagements, and regulatory compliance across direct tax, indirect tax, audit, and financial reporting.

01

Audit & Assurance

Independent examination of financial statements and internal controls, conducted in accordance with applicable Standards on Auditing issued by ICAI.

  • Statutory Audit under Companies Act
  • Tax Audit under Section 44AB
  • Internal & Management Audit
  • Concurrent Audit
  • Stock Audit
02

Direct Taxation

Preparation, filing, and advisory on income tax matters for individuals, firms, companies, and trusts under the Income-tax Act, 1961.

  • ITR Filing — All Categories
  • Corporate & Individual Tax Planning
  • TDS Compliance & Returns
  • Capital Gains & Property Tax Advisory
  • Advance Tax Calculations & Planning
  • Assessment & Representation
03

GST & Indirect Tax

End-to-end GST compliance — from registration to annual returns — along with advisory on classification, input tax credit, and departmental proceedings.

  • GST Registration & Amendment
  • Monthly / Quarterly Returns
  • GSTR-9 & GSTR-9C Filing
  • ITC Reconciliation
  • GST Audit & Representation
04

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Supervision and maintenance of accounting books, ledger entries, and financial records in compliance with current accounting principles.

  • Day-to-day Bookkeeping & Accounting
  • Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS)
  • Preparation of Trial Balance & Ledgers
  • Year-End Closing & Adjustment Entries
  • Supervision of Accounting Staff
05

Financial Reporting

Preparation and review of financial statements under applicable accounting standards, including Ind AS, Companies Act Schedule III, and standalone IGAAP.

  • Ind AS Financial Statements
  • MRL / Management Representations
  • CARO 2020 Reporting
  • Projected Financials
  • Cash Flow & Net Worth Statements
06

Business Advisory

Practical guidance on business structure, regulatory compliances, MSME registrations, and operational financial matters for growing enterprises.

  • Udyam & MSME Registration
  • Company Incorporation
  • MCA / ROC Compliances
  • Project Report Preparation
  • Business Structuring Advisory
07

Certifications

Issuance of certificates and reports required for regulatory filings, bank submissions, government applications, and compliance purposes.

  • Net Worth Certificates
  • Income Certificates
  • Form 15CA / 15CB (FEMA)
  • Turnover Certificates
  • Statutory Compliance Certificates
08

Business Valuations & Projections

Independent valuation of business entities and preparation of financial projections for investment, lending, restructuring, and strategic decision-making purposes.

  • Business & Enterprise Valuation
  • Projected Financial Statements
  • Cash Flow & Profitability Projections
  • Valuation for M&A / Fundraising
  • Bank / Investor Report Preparation
